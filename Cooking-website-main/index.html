Delicious Chapati

Ingredients:

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Cooking Oil

Steps:

  1. Mix flour and salt
  2. Add warm water and knead into a soft dough
  3. Divide into small portions
  4. Roll and cook on a pan
  5. Apply oil until golden brown

Crispy Chicken Nuggets

Ingredients:

  • 1kg boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 4 large eggs, beaten
  • 4 cups baking flour for coating
  • 6 tablespoons garlic salt
  • 3 tablespoons black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon paprika
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Steps:

  1. Heat oil while mixing flour, garlic salt, and pepper
  2. Whisk the eggs in a separate bowl
  3. Dip chicken in egg, then coat in flour mixture
  4. Fry in hot oil until golden brown and crispy

Crispy French Fries

Ingredients:

  • Large potatoes
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper (optional)
  • Paprika (optional)
  • Garlic powder (optional)

Steps:

  1. Cut potatoes into even strips
  2. Soak in cold water for 30-60 minutes
  3. Dry completely
  4. First fry (5-7 mins) until soft
  5. Rest for 10-15 minutes
  6. Second fry (2-4 mins) until golden and crispy
  7. Season immediately with salt, pepper, paprika, and garlic powder
