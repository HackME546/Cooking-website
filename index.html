    • Crispy Chicken nugged

    Ingredients

  • 1.1kg boneless skinless chicken breasts cut in small chunks
    2.4 large eggs, beaten for binder
    3.4 cups of backing flour for coating
    4.6 tablespoons of garlic salt
    5.3 tablespoons of black papper
    6.1 tablespoons of paprica colour (optional)
    7.Vegetable oil for frying

    • Steps

  • *Heat the oil while you mix flour, garlic salt and pepper in a bowel
    *Whisk the eggs in a separate bowel
    *Dip the chicken into the egg, then toss into the flour mixture until well coated
    *Dip in the hot oil to cook until golden brown and Crispy
